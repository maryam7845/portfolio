🛒 Amazon PPC Campaign Plan (Concept)

A sample structure for launching a new handmade candle product on Amazon. This shows how I would organise the campaigns.

Step 1

Auto Campaign

Let Amazon find search terms, then collect the ones that get clicks and orders.

Step 2

Manual Campaign

Move proven keywords to exact/phrase match for tighter control over spend.

Step 3

Negative Keywords

Block irrelevant searches that waste budget without converting.

Step 4

Review & Optimise

Track ACoS, clicks and orders weekly, then adjust bids and budget.
